About exceptional approval

Turn this on for products that need a senior sign-off.

  • Reviewers and Approvers work as usual.
  • The decision then waits in the Executive's queue.
  • The borrower is only emailed after the Executive approves.

Auto approval

The scheduled job only approves applications up to the max amount, and signs the letters with the selected user's signature.
