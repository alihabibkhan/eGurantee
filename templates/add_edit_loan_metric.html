How the matrix is used

When an application is reviewed, the row matching its product, occupation and experience range sets the limits checked in the review pop-up's KFT matrix tab.

  • Required paid off: the share of the current loan that must be repaid before a repeat loan.
  • Repeat increment: how much larger a repeat loan may be than the previous one.
  • Global loan ceiling: the maximum loan for this row.
